Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Discord bot "meow" ("the Bot", "we", "us") handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect and Store

The Bot does not collect or persistently store any personal data. We do not maintain a database of users, servers, messages, or any other information.

2. Information Processed Temporarily

To respond to commands, the Bot receives data from Discord (such as the command you run and the IDs of the user, server, and channel it was used in). This data is processed in memory only to carry out your request and is discarded afterwards. It is not saved, logged long-term, or linked to you.

3. Message Content

The Bot does not read, store, or analyze the content of your messages beyond what is strictly needed to respond to a command you invoke.

4. Sharing and Selling of Data

Because we do not store personal data, we have nothing to sell or share. We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties or use it for advertising or profiling.

5. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates on Discord. Your use of Discord is governed by Discord's Privacy Policy. The Bot may be hosted on third-party infrastructure that processes network requests as part of normal operation.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

As no personal data is persistently stored, there is no data to retain or delete. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will investigate and remove it.

7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest revision.
